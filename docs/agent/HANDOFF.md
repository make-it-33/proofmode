## Opportunity and capture plan

Casefile can make ProofMode recognizable by turning evidence—the thing the game measures—into the emotional and visual center. The app can feel like uncovering a consequential case rather than completing another productivity exercise.

Protect the opportunity by keeping the real product visible, avoiding crime/hacker clichés, keeping copy short, and using media only where it clarifies a mission or replay.
