## May proceed without another prompt

Dependency-ordered page implementation matching canonical specs; guided checkpoint; private result/replay; truthful local-progress foundations; Profile/Settings scaffolds; routine approved UX polish; accessibility/security/privacy/performance/test/documentation/CI repair; reversible refactoring; branch updates; PR readiness; green merge; issue/handoff/run-log/ADR hygiene.

Make the safest professional decision, document it, test it, and merge it.

## Explicit gates

New approval is required for personal-data collection/public regions/consent changes; paid or lock-in backend/AI/analytics/moderation/payment/signing/distribution vendors; production spend/credentials/uploads/code execution; public profiles/discovery/messaging/contact upload/youth-to-adult policy; any reintroduction of competition/rank/leaderboard/matchmaking/prizes/cross-user scoring; pricing/checkout/employer/school/hiring/certification; signed native release/auto-update/destructive migration/production deletion; material audience/positioning/name/Proof Chain/website-app change.

When blocked, keep behavior disabled and continue safe reversible foundations. Never fabricate completion.

## Prohibited claims and mechanics

No fake availability, metric, rank, user count, testimonial, installer, update, AI call, submission, profile, persistence, payment, or security claim. No pay-to-win, public-by-default learner data, manipulative streak/energy system, competition, or cross-user comparison.

## Record discipline

Append every new decision with date, exact scope/exclusions, and reviewed artifact; update conflicting canonical specs in the same change. Keep private conversation and personal information out of the repository; capture durable intent in structured documents.
