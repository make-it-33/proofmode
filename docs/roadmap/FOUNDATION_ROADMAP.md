# Foundation roadmap

## Phase 0 — foundation (complete)

Repository governance, agent instructions, CI/security baseline, mission contract, deterministic scoring spike, corrected product rules/architecture, researched backlog, Make the call behavior, Casefile direction, and a 13+ learner-first audience sequence.

Exit: fixtures pass, one mission is unambiguous on paper, and the vertical-slice contract is agreed.

## Phase 1 — trustworthy learner vertical slice

Ages 13+ learner product; age-aware guest/auth path; high-privacy defaults; immutable registry; one production mission; trusted event log; bounded AI gateway/mock; evidence-linked submission; deterministic explanation; private replay/result card without fake percentiles; accessibility/security/privacy/telemetry/E2E.

Exit: 20 representative blind players; at least 80% agree scoring is fair; no answer cues or known exploits; youth privacy, age/consent, deletion/export, provider, accessibility, and abuse gates pass for selected launch regions.

## Phase 2 — reusable game platform

Five mechanic renderers, content studio/QA workflow, 10–15 excellent missions, provisional skill profile, cost/performance budgets, and isolated disposable sandbox foundations before any code/build mission.

## Phase 3 — social retention

Daily scheduling, same-version challenges, decision-difference replay, seasons/leagues/streaks/notifications with anti-abuse, wellbeing, age-aware privacy, and data-backed share cards.

## Phase 4 — paid learner value and hardening

Professional learner packs, deep replay, weakness analysis, billing/entitlements, deletion/export/support/incident tooling, security/load/backup/privacy readiness.

## Phase 5 — separately validated employer exploration

Only after a new owner-approved product gate: job-specific validity research, adverse-impact and accessibility analysis, candidate rights, separate employer authorization/data/audit boundaries, legal review, and a tightly bounded pilot. No automatic hiring decisions.

Consider native only after web retention, notification value, and a mobile-specific need are proven.
