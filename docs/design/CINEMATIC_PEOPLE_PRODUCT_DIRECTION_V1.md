# Cinematic people + product direction v1

Status: **selected for detailed exploration only**  
Selected: 2026-08-18 by the owner  
Run ID: `2026-08-18-cinematic-people-product-selection`

## Decision

Cinematic people + product leads the next ProofMode creative reset.

This selection authorizes a detailed desktop/mobile approval pack and concept media. It does not authorize production UI changes, final asset adoption, or materially new onboarding behavior.

## Intended feeling

- mature, ambitious, and credible;
- human capability amplified by AI, not generic AI spectacle;
- cinematic enough to create desire, restrained enough to feel like a real product;
- competitive and consequential without childish badges, fake terminals, or fabricated status;
- visually led, with short natural copy and clear product action.

## Core composition hypothesis

The first screen should pair one concise promise and one primary action with one strong cinematic human moment integrated with real ProofMode product behavior.

The media should show a player moving from an AI claim to evidence, challenge, correction, and a defensible lock. It should not be a decorative montage or a substitute for the product.

## Onboarding hypothesis

- no permanent guide layer on every screen;
- first-use onboarding is staged and skippable;
- each mechanic is introduced only when first needed;
- later runs begin directly with the mission;
- essential instructions remain available on demand;
- progress and consequence are shown through state change rather than explanatory paragraphs.

## Media exploration boundary

Allowed for the approval pack:

- original concept stills;
- a short muted concept loop or storyboard;
- product-interface motion integrated with the human visual;
- static mobile and reduced-motion fallbacks;
- clearly labeled non-production imagery.

Required before any production use:

- owner approval of exact assets and motion;
- provenance and licensing record;
- responsive crops and focal-point rules;
- alt text or decorative treatment;
- captions/transcript for meaningful video;
- poster frame and no-media fallback;
- loading strategy and measured file budget;
- reduced-motion equivalent;
- no essential interaction dependent on media.

## Functional continuity

Retain for detailed exploration:

- `Scout -> Challenge -> Lock -> Result`;
- source -> claim -> verification -> decision proof chain;
- explicit fallible AI move;
- visible recovery;
- deliberate lock;
- private six-dimension debrief and pivotal replay;
- privacy and browser-boundary contracts.

## Avoid

- childish arcade posters, sticker-like pills, and novelty accents as the identity;
- repeated guide copy, card walls, and marketing-stat rows;
- generic AI gradients, glowing brains, robots, hologram hands, fake terminals, or random code rain;
- lifestyle photography disconnected from the actual mission;
- autoplay audio or interaction-blocking video;
- fabricated users, experts, employers, ranks, testimonials, or outcome claims;
- implementing concept media directly in production before review.

## Detailed pack requirement

The next review artifact must show desktop and 390 px mobile for:

1. cinematic first visit;
2. staged first-use onboarding;
3. mission entry and clock start;
4. Scout with minimal guidance;
5. Challenge and source inspection;
6. recovery;
7. Lock;
8. Result and replay;
9. loading/no-media/reduced-motion treatment;
10. visual, type, motion, media, accessibility, provenance, and performance system.

The owner must approve that exact pack before production implementation.
