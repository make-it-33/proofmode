# Market and competition research

Updated: 2026-08-15

## Demand

- Gallup reported in January 2026 that 26% of U.S. employees used AI at work at least a few times a week, while 49% never did. Frequent use in remote-capable roles reached 40%; leaders used AI more than individual contributors. Gallup identifies lack of utility as a common barrier. This supports short role-relevant practice, not generic lessons. Source: https://www.gallup.com/workplace/701195/frequent-workplace-continued-rise.aspx
- McKinsey’s 2025 workplace report says 46% of leaders identify workforce skill gaps as a significant AI-adoption barrier. Source: https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/superagency-in-the-workplace-empowering-people-to-unlock-ais-full-potential-at-work
- A 2025 study developed scales for collaborative AI literacy and metacognition, supporting the importance of monitoring/regulating collaboration—not only tool knowledge. Source: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10447318.2025.2543997
- OWASP’s 2025 GenAI list highlights prompt injection, disclosure, output handling, excessive agency, misinformation, and unbounded consumption. Source: https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/

## Competition

| Category | Examples | Strength | ProofMode gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hiring assessments | Canditech, iMocha, Vervoe, Adaface | Practical simulations | Employer-first, episodic, stressful |
| AI literacy tests | AISA, JobCannon | Clear frameworks/baselines | Often test-shaped, not rich collaboration |
| Gamified AI learning | SkillFlow, Coursiv | Habit and progression | Teaches tools more than judgment |
| Cognitive/AI arenas | AIHumanBench, AI battle experiments | Competition/shareability | Generic cognition, weak workflow transfer |
| Technical competition | Kaggle, LeetCode, HackerRank | Strong identity/rankings | Long, technical, narrow audience |

## Wedge

A daily consumer competition measuring evidence-backed human judgment while AI is available. The defensible asset is the authored mission/truth-map library, deterministic scoring/replays, longitudinal skill data, fair same-version competition, and a shareable identity—not the chat UI.
