# ADR 0005: Casefile leads detailed experience exploration

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-16
- Owner decision: Gate 2 approval

## Context

After approving Make the call as the first core behavior, ProofMode needed a coherent creative center before detailed UX work.

Three materially different directions were presented:

- **Casefile** — human, tactile, investigative.
- **Live Case** — shared, competitive, broadcast-like.
- **Workbench** — calm, precise, product-native.

The first Signal Ops pack had too much on-screen text, weak appeal, and AI-sounding vocabulary. The replacement pack showed actual product moments with short copy and tested each direction at desktop and mobile sizes.

## Decision

Use **Casefile** as the creative direction for detailed Make the call exploration.

Casefile will:

1. make evidence—not AI imagery—the visual identity;
2. pair warm paper/document surfaces with a graphite field;
3. use restrained acid lime for player focus and primary action, never pre-submit truth;
4. use editorial typography only for emotional framing and clear sans/mono typography inside the product;
5. keep the evidence workspace, AI availability, submission contract, and uncertainty legible;
6. feel human and consequential without turning into crime-board or terminal theater.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Gives the app and website a recognizable identity rooted in the product behavior.
- Directly answers the owner’s request for less text and a more appealing, non-generic feel.
- Supports mission-specific visual storytelling without requiring robots, glowing brains, or generic AI gradients.
- Can create a coherent bridge between marketing, missions, evidence, replay, and share cards.

### Negative / risks

- Physical evidence metaphors can become theatrical, decorative, or genre-specific.
- Generated imagery can introduce fake text, visual inaccuracies, licensing ambiguity, or an artificial feel.
- Editorial typography can harm product clarity if used inside dense interfaces.
- Dark fields and lime accents can become another generic tech treatment if overused.

### Controls

- Product behavior stays visible; imagery never replaces the real mission interface.
- Evidence is answer-neutral before submission.
- Lime is reserved for player focus, primary action, and verified post-submit relationships.
- No red-string boards, hacker costumes, fake terminals, robots, brains, holograms, or permanent glow.
- Product copy is short, direct, and human.
- Every generated or sourced asset remains exploration until separately approved with provenance, licensing, alt treatment, responsive crops, and byte budgets.
- Gate 3 must include desktop/mobile, state coverage, accessibility, performance, and reduced-motion evidence.

## Alternatives

- **Live Case:** retained as a reference for timing, daily competition, and replay energy; not approved as the leading direction.
- **Workbench:** retained as the clarity floor and possible professional-pack influence; not approved as the leading direction.

Neither alternative is approved for detailed direction work unless the owner reopens Gate 2.

## Scope boundary

This decision authorizes only:

- detailed desktop/mobile Make the call journey exploration;
- loading, empty, AI, decision, timeout, error, completion, score, replay, and recovery states;
- a proposed token/component system;
- motion/media storyboard, accessibility plan, and performance budget;
- a separate technical-stack comparison.

It does not authorize production implementation, final copy, production media, framework/vendor selection, backend work, rankings, social systems, monetization, native apps, public launch, or use of exploratory images in a production bundle.
